CIA Triad stands for Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, which are the three main objectives of any security professional.
[[Confidentiality]]: is the concept that information must be accessed by only authorized users.
[[Integrity]]: means that information aren't tempered with or modified by an undesirable party.
[[Availability]]: means that the information is always available/ accessible by authorized parties.
