Confidentiality is the assurance that only authorized users can access information and resources.
### Risks against confidentiality
1- Snooping: means that a threat roaming inside any facility (Office, Factory...etc) looking for critical information like papers left alone on top of a desk.
Protection against snooping can be enforcing policies that ensure information are protected like:
- Clean Desk: Employees must make sure that they put away any document from workspace when they step away even if it's just for a moment.

2- Dumpster diving: Malicious parties can dive inside the trash of an organization looking for any documents that may help them gather sensitive information.
Protection against this type of attack can be achieved by using shredders when they discard them, at least this will make it impossible for the attacker to reform the sensitive documents again.

3- Eavesdropping: Comes in both Physical and Electronic types, 
	- Physical would be the Attacker positioning themselves where they can overhear conversations waiting for the moment when the target slips his tongue and mentions some sensitive info, this can be anywhere the target can be at like in coffee shops, clubs...etc
		- Protection against this type of attack should be by putting rules where sensitive conversations should take place like in closed office, conference room...etc  
	- Electronic aka wiretapping, it happens when an attacker gains access to a network and starts monitoring these data.
		- Protection against this type can be achieved thanks to encryption which limits the attackers ability to gain info or at least make it hard for the to decrypt them.
4- Social Engineering: it happens when the attacker use Psychological tricks to deceive employees into giving up sensitive info, it can be through emails, or in person, where the attacker impersonates one that have access.
- Protection: it is hard to protect against this types of attacks, the best defense is to educate the stuff to recognize the dangers of this attack, and empower them to intervene when they suspect an attack is taking place.
