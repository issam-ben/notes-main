Privacy is the rights and abilities of a person to control how information about them is stored, shared, and used.

## Types of private information

Two most common elements of private information are:
 [[Personally identifiable information PII]] : includes all the info that can be tied back to an individual.
 [[Protected Health Information PHI]]: Includes health care records,...
## Expectation of Privacy
Privacy programs are based on a legal principle known as [[Reasonable Expectation of Privacy]] which means that based on an individual act we can determine whether they are expecting privacy or not, like someone posting a public post on social media, in which he doesn't expect privacy in contrast with someone sending an email, or providing tax information to a government's agency website, he would expect much higher privacy. 

## Privacy Management Framework PMF
[see for more](https://us.aicpa.org/interestareas/informationtechnology/privacy-management-framework)

it is a privacy framework developed by AICPA American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and other experts in the field, it aims to establish a global framework for privacy management, it includes  principles as follows:
1. Management
2. Agreement, notice, and communication
3. Collection and Creation
4. Use, retention, and disposal
5. Access
6. Disclosure to third parties
7. Security for privacy
8. Data integrity and quality
9. Monitoring and enforcement
