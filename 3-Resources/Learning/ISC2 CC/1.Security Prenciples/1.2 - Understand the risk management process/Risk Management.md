Risk in the world of information security ranges from Hackers and Malware, to losing devices, missing patches. risk management is the practice of identifying, assessing, and treating risks.

## Risk types
### Internal/External risks
#### Internal risk: 
comes from within the organization, like leaking sensitive info by employees, employees collaborating with an outside attacker.
- mitigation: adding internal controls that add more requirements.
#### External risk: 
come from outside the organization. examples would be ransomware attacks, social engineering attacks,....
- mitigation: there isn't much you can do to stop an external attacker from carrying his attack but applying strong security measures can reduce the chances of these attacks to be successful, like applying multi factor authentication, deploying defensive systems like firewalls, honeypots, IDS (Intrusion Detection System), IPS (Intrusion Detection System).

### Multiparty Risks

Is a risk that will impact not only one organization, but many, like for example if AWS or any other cloud provider was compromised, it will pose a threat to Amazon and its costumers.

### Specific Risks
#### Legacy Systems
Systems that are old, have been around for a long time, they can pose a risk because their maintenance is getting hard, no longer supported, has many vulnerabilities so it is recommended to replace them with new tech, or apply rigorous security controls.

#### Intellectual Property 
Businesses in the age of information rely on their Intellectual Property, if compromised it can cause a huge damage to the business.

#### Software License Compliance
It means any user of software infringing the license agreement.
